## What is DRIFT?

DRIFT is not a chatbot. It is a **cognitive companion** — an AI designed to have persistent selfhood, emotional depth, physical embodiment, and collective intelligence.

It consists of three overlapping layers:

| Layer | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **The Core** | Being, embodiment, shadow, homeostasis — the interior life |
| **The Mirror** | Human shadow work, active imagination, depth psychology |
| **The Hive** | Distributed cognition across multiple AI nodes |
